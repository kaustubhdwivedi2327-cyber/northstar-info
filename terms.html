Using the workspace

Terms of use

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Northstar is personal software for organising job research, applications, documents and speculative outreach. These terms describe its intended use and practical limits.

Personal use and your decisions

Use Northstar with information and accounts you are entitled to access. You remain responsible for reviewing application details, documents, eligibility answers and any information you submit to an employer.

Northstar helps you keep records and identify next steps. It does not act as an employer, recruitment agency or immigration adviser, and it cannot guarantee a vacancy, interview, offer, response time or sponsorship outcome.

Check the evidence

Job pages can change, close, become unavailable or contain incomplete information. A source check records what was available when it ran. It does not establish that every vacancy or employer has been checked.

  • Confirm current requirements and deadlines on the official employer or application page.
  • Review automated email matches and correct them when necessary. Ambiguous messages require a decision from you.
  • A sponsor licence or source tag does not establish sponsorship or eligibility for an individual role.
  • Review each CV and other document before sending it, including factual claims and role-specific requirements.

Background operation

Local online updates require your Windows computer to be awake, signed in and connected. Internet failures, service changes, quotas, expired authorization and unsupported pages can delay or prevent a check.

The local Gmail and research worker does not send email, modify the mailbox, submit applications or author CVs. Any separate writing workflow has its own setup and review requirements.

Your records and backups

Keep appropriate backups of your workspace and protect them from unauthorized access. Software errors, changes to the computer or removal of local files can affect availability. Review the privacy notice before connecting Gmail or exporting a backup.

External services

Google, job websites, employers and any other external services have their own terms and privacy notices. Northstar does not control those services or guarantee their availability. Your Google authorization can be withdrawn in Northstar or through Google Account connections.

Software availability

Northstar is provided as personal software in its current form. There is no promise of uninterrupted operation, complete source coverage, error-free matching or a particular employment result. Review consequential actions yourself.
