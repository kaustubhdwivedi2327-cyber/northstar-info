Personal desktop software

Your next move,
thoughtfully organised.

Northstar brings job applications, preparation and follow-ups into one private workspace on your Windows computer.

How Northstar handles your data
01 / A CLEAR VIEW

Keep every country in focus.

Organise vacancy applications by country. Track speculative company outreach separately, with its own contacts, stages and follow-ups.

02 / UPDATES WITH EVIDENCE

Bring application emails together.

Optionally connect Gmail with read-only permission. Clear, unique application matches can update a stage; ambiguous messages wait for your review.

03 / A DAILY CHECK

Return to fresher research.

A background worker checks a rotating batch of supported official job sources and records what it could verify, including blocked pages and uncertain results.

On your computer

A workspace you control.

Northstar’s application records, documents and selected email evidence are stored locally. Optional Windows startup keeps background checks available while you are signed in.

  • The computer must be awake and connected for online updates. The app window can be closed.
  • Gmail updates use the account you explicitly authorize through Google. You can disconnect it in Northstar.
  • Applications and outreach remain under your control. The worker does not submit forms or send email.
  • Job availability, sponsorship and eligibility are checked against evidence; uncertain details remain visible for review.
This website provides information about Northstar. It is separate from the desktop workspace and does not provide access to application records, CVs or email. Read the privacy notice for the complete data flow and the terms of use for practical limitations.
